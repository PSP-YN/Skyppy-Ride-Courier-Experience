Privacy notice

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This website is an independently created design prototype for a product-design challenge. It is not operated by Skyppy Logistics Pvt. Ltd. and is not connected to Skyppy's customer or driver systems.

1. Information handled by this prototype

The prototype uses fictional sample names, routes, fares, shipment references and PINs. When you interact with it, information you type or actions you take may be kept temporarily in the page or in local browser storage on your device to demonstrate a workflow. The notification centre and support-draft feature store their demo entries in local storage. They do not submit those entries to a server.

2. Notifications

Browser notifications are off unless you explicitly enable them using the notification control and grant browser permission. Any notification generated by this prototype is local to this browser session/device. This site does not register a remote push-notification subscription or send background push messages.

3. Location, payments and identity

The prototype does not request device location, contact real drivers, dispatch parcels, authenticate accounts, send SMS/OTP messages, process payments, or connect to a payment provider. Maps and route illustrations are schematic. Do not enter real passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, precise addresses, phone numbers or sensitive personal information.

4. Sharing and third parties

The prototype does not intentionally transmit form entries or local support drafts to Skyppy or another service. The website is hosted by Vercel and source code is hosted by GitHub; those providers may process standard request and security logs under their own policies. Links to third-party sites are governed by those sites' terms and privacy notices.

5. Retention and deletion

Demo data stored by the prototype remains in your browser's local storage until you clear the site's data or use the notification centre's clear control. Clearing browser site data removes locally stored demo entries. Private browsing modes and browser settings may change persistence behavior.

6. Children and sensitive data

This is a public design demonstration, not a service intended to collect personal data from children. Do not submit real personal or sensitive information.

7. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when the prototype changes. The project does not provide an operational customer-support channel. Use the support page only to prepare a local demo draft, or return to the interactive prototype.

Important: This notice describes the current prototype behavior, not the privacy practices of Skyppy or any future production service. A real launch requires a separate legal review, data map, retention policy, processor agreements and privacy notice from the service operator.
