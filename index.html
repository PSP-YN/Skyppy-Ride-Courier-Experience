RIDE BOOKING

Less uncertainty at every step.

From destination search to post-trip rating, prioritize comprehension, trust and control.

01 / CHOOSE

Set your route

Recognizable pickup and destination fields, recent places and location confirmation reduce avoidable mistakes.

02 / COMPARE

See the full fare

Vehicle type, arrival estimate and fare are presented together before the rider commits.

03 / TRAVEL

Stay informed and safe

Driver details, trip sharing, SOS, support and post-trip feedback are placed at relevant moments.

INTERCITY COURIER & LOGISTICS

Every parcel has a clear next step.

Sender, receiver and business teams get a consistent view—from pickup scheduling to proof of delivery.

01 / PREPARE

Describe the parcel

Package type, weight, declared value, fragile handling and pickup time before the estimate.

02 / FOLLOW

Track meaningful events

Pickup confirmed, in transit, out for delivery and delivered are distinct states. Exceptions explain the next action.

03 / CONFIRM

Close the delivery loop

Receiver confirmation and proof of delivery, with a clear support path if delivery fails.
