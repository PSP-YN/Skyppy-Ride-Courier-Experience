# Skyppy — Clarity for Every Journey

**Independent UI/UX concept · Ride booking + intercity courier**

## Submission summary
A research-led mobility and logistics experience concept built around one principle: users should understand the cost, status, responsibility and next action at every step. It covers the passenger journey from route selection and itemized fare review through driver matching, safety, payment and rating; the sender journey for intercity parcels; separate driver and receiver workflows; and a desktop SMB shipment workspace.

The design emphasizes transparent estimates, explicit operational states, recoverable exceptions, role-specific workflows, responsive mobile-first layouts, low-data behavior and English/Kannada labels on key fields.

## Challenge coverage
- **Ride:** pickup/drop, route preview, vehicle comparison, itemized estimate, matching, driver details, trip states, safety/support preview, payment simulation and rating.
- **Driver:** request review, accept/decline, arrival, trip start and completion.
- **Courier:** parcel type, weight, fragile/high-value handling, declared value, pickup schedule, estimate, tracking events and exception recovery.
- **Receiver:** physical-handover acknowledgement, condition check, demo PIN and local proof-of-delivery preview.
- **SMB:** shipment overview, filters, local CSV import/export and sample record creation.

## Research and originality
Desk research covers Skyppy's public Android customer and Driver Partner listings plus public competitor review evidence from Uber, Ola, Rapido, Namma Yatri, Porter, Delhivery and Shiprocket. India-focused consumer surveys and app-quality review samples inform the design decisions. Sources, sample sizes and caveats are in the public case study. No competitor screens are copied.

## Honest scope
This is an interactive design prototype, not a live transport platform. Fares, identities, routes, PINs and shipment rows are fictional. There is no live dispatch, GPS, payment, authentication, OTP, persistent database or courier integration. No user interviews or moderated usability-test results are claimed.
