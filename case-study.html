PRODUCT DESIGN CHALLENGE · CASE STUDY

Make every journey
feel clear and in control.

A unified experience concept for Skyppy ride booking and intercity courier journeys, designed around trust, understandable decisions and useful status updates.

Scope · Ride + courierPlatforms · Mobile + desktopOutput · UX flows + interactive concept

01 · The problem

Mobility and parcel delivery are multi-step services where uncertainty creates friction. Riders need to know pickup, fare and driver details. Senders need to understand handling, pickup timing, delivery progress and what happens when something goes wrong. Small businesses need to review many shipments without opening each one.

The design goal is to make the next action obvious, explain costs before commitment and keep reassurance available without overwhelming the task.

02 · Users and needs

Everyday rider

  • First-time or infrequent app user
  • Clear pickup confirmation
  • Fare and arrival estimate before booking
  • Visible trip sharing and emergency support

Sender, receiver and SMB

  • Clear package and handling choices
  • Scheduled pickup and meaningful tracking
  • Delivery confirmation and proof
  • Multi-shipment visibility for business

Design consideration: low literacy, low-end devices, weak networks, regional language support and accessible touch targets are core requirements, not edge cases.

03 · Experience decisions

One decision at a time

Separate pickup, destination, ride selection and confirmation so users can review the route before committing.

Transparent estimates

Show ride type, arrival estimate and fare together. For courier, show handling and estimated price before confirmation.

State-based tracking

Use clear states such as assigned, picked up, in transit, out for delivery and delivered. Exceptions need a reason and next action.

Contextual safety

Keep SOS, trip sharing, verified driver information, support and proof of delivery discoverable at the relevant moment.

04 · Screens and prototype

  1. Ride booking — mobile destination entry, map preview, ride options and fare CTA.
  2. Courier booking — mobile route, package type, handling details, pickup schedule and estimate.
  3. Business logistics — desktop shipment overview, status summary and multi-shipment table.

The deployed prototype adds interactive ride and parcel forms. It demonstrates form state and indicative estimates only; it does not call live dispatch, maps, pricing, payments or tracking.

05 · Accessibility and resilience

  • High-contrast text, visible focus states and large touch targets.
  • Plain-language labels and English/Kannada entry point.
  • Important states expressed with text, not color alone.
  • Production should preserve entered details and show last confirmed status offline.

06 · Scope honesty

This is an independent challenge concept, not a production Skyppy service. Sample fares, routes and dashboard metrics are illustrative UI content. No driver matching, live location, payment, dispatch, proof-of-delivery storage or account system is connected. A production release requires verified tariffs, service coverage, operational APIs, safety escalation, localization review and usability testing.

07 · Validation plan

  1. Test with first-time riders, parcel senders and small-business operators.
  2. Measure booking completion, fare comprehension, route correction and exception recovery.
  3. Test weak-network and low-end Android performance.
  4. Validate Kannada copy, accessibility and safety escalation with local users.
  5. Connect approved operational APIs only after service requirements are confirmed.
